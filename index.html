How to Use Control Insight Studio

1. Select a Plant: Choose a standard transfer function or build a "Custom" one by typing comma-separated coefficients.

2. Tune PID Gains: Adjust the Kp, Ki, Kd sliders.

3. Analyze Plots:
- Step Response: Shows transient time-domain performance.
- Bode Plots: Monitor Frequency domain stability (Mag and Phase stacked on the right).
